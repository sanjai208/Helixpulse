STEP 04 · CONCEPTS

Computational Biology Notes

Key concepts and methodological boundaries for your assignment write-up.

01

GC Content Ratio

Calculated as ((G + C) / Length) × 100. GC base pairs form three hydrogen bonds compared to two in AT pairs, influencing DNA thermal stability and serving as a classic baseline genomic feature.

02

Small-Sample Overfitting

With only four labelled training sequences (S1–S4), a single split at 56.25% achieves 100% training separation. However, four observations cannot establish statistical generalization to unseen genomes.

03

Companion Scope

This web interface serves as an interactive visual aid alongside your required Python / Google Colab DecisionTreeClassifier implementation. Not intended for clinical or diagnostic decisions.
